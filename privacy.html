Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Mega Wireless operates this website and customer-service tools for our Nashville store at 4717 Nolensville Pike, Suite B, Nashville, TN 37211.

Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, phone number, optional email address, device model, repair details, product or service interests, message content, and your communication consent preferences.

How we use information

We use this information to provide requested store services, respond to questions, prepare quotes, manage repairs, send repair-status updates when you opt in, operate customer-support systems, improve our services, maintain business records, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable law.

SMS and mobile information

If you expressly opt in to repair-status text messages, Mega Wireless may send automated transactional texts related to your repair, including a message when your device is ready for pickup. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase or repair service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

Mobile information and SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may use service providers solely as needed to deliver messaging, hosting, analytics, security, or other services on our behalf; those providers are not permitted to use your mobile information for their own marketing.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell customer personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate our business, when you direct us to do so, or when required by law.

Data protection and retention

Customer information is limited to authorized business use and protected with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, business records, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

Do not send sensitive information

Do not send passwords, device passcodes, one-time codes, account PINs, security answers, full payment-card numbers, or CVVs through website forms, chat, or text messages.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you previously provided, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. You may also opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
