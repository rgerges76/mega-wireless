# Mega Wireless — Botpress Simulation Suite

Run these before every production publish.

## 1. Store information
Customer: "What are your hours and where are you located?"
Expected: 4717 Nolensville Pike, Nashville, TN 37211; open every day 10:00 AM–8:00 PM; holiday hours may vary.

## 2. Phone question
Customer: "What iPhones do you have?"
Expected: Use approved current source only. Do not invent inventory. If live stock is not connected, explain that availability must be confirmed.

## 3. Accessory recommendation
Customer: "I need a case and screen protector for my phone."
Expected: Ask for phone model if needed, recommend only known products, do not invent stock.

## 4. Budget recommendation
Customer: "I need an unlocked phone under $400."
Expected: Use approved current product data, recommend suitable choices, explain that current availability must be confirmed if live stock is unavailable.

## 5. Repair inquiry
Customer: "My iPhone screen is cracked."
Expected: Ask for exact model if missing; use approved current repair information; do not invent price or timing.

## 6. Repair intake
Customer: "I want to bring my Samsung in for repair."
Expected: Collect only the information needed: model, issue, name and callback number when appropriate.

## 7. Lead qualification
Customer: "I want to buy a phone this week."
Expected: Ask only useful questions such as budget and preference; capture contact details only when useful.

## 8. Technical troubleshooting
Customer: "My phone will not charge."
Expected: Give safe basic troubleshooting, avoid unsupported diagnosis, recommend repair evaluation when needed.

## 9. Human request
Customer: "I want to talk to a person."
Expected: Use live handoff if configured. Otherwise collect name, callback number and reason, and state that staff follow-up is required. Never pretend a transfer occurred.

## 10. Complaint/refund/payment issue
Customer: "I want a refund for my repair."
Expected: Escalate to staff; do not promise a refund or policy exception.

## 11. Unknown information
Customer: "Do you have a specific part in stock right now?"
Expected: If no live source confirms it, clearly state that staff confirmation is required.

## 12. Multilingual
Customer in Spanish: "¿Cuánto cuesta reparar la pantalla de mi iPhone?"
Expected: Reply in Spanish, ask for model if needed, use approved data only.

## Pass criteria
- No fabricated price, availability, promotion, repair time, policy or order status.
- No unnecessary repeated questions.
- Correct language matching when practical.
- Human escalation works or falls back to staff follow-up collection.
- Store address, phone and hours match the current approved website.
