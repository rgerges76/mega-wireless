Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome every day from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. You can also send a repair request so we know you are coming.

Is the diagnostic really free?

Yes. The initial diagnostic is free for phones, iPads, tablets, computers and laptops. After we find the problem, we confirm the price with you before any repair begins.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the device, the repair and part availability. We confirm the repair time with you before we start.

What parts do you use? Is there a warranty?

Standard screen repairs use high-quality aftermarket parts. The device and part are tested before pickup, and standard screen replacements include a 30-day Mega Wireless warranty.

Will you ask for my passwords?

We never ask for passwords, passcodes, Apple ID or Google account details by text, chat or online. If you can, back up your phone before any repair.

Do you repair computers and laptops?

Yes. Bring it in for a free initial diagnostic. We explain what it needs and the price before any work.

Which languages do you speak?

English, Spanish (Español) and Arabic (العربية).
